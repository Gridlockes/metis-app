Metis

A self-hosted personal dashboard.

Metis is a private dashboard that runs on its owner's own computer. It brings notes, reminders, mail, files, flowcharts and home devices together in one place. It is not a hosted service: there are no accounts to sign up for, and nobody else's data passes through it.

Google Calendar

Metis can connect to the owner's Google Calendar so that:

  • reminders set in Metis also appear as events in Google Calendar;
  • renaming, moving or deleting one of those events in Google updates the reminder in Metis;
  • upcoming Google Calendar events are shown on the Metis calendar page and can be read aloud by Metis's greeter at home.

How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
