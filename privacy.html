Privacy policy

Metis · Effective 6 October 2026

Metis is a personal dashboard that its owner runs on their own computer. This policy explains what Metis does with data from a connected Google account. Metis is not offered as a service to the public, and it is used only by the person who runs it.

What Metis accesses

When you connect Google Calendar, Metis asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events. With it, Metis can:

  • read event titles, descriptions, locations, start and end times, and links in your calendar;
  • create, update and delete events that mirror reminders you set in Metis. Each of these events is tagged with the reminder it belongs to.

Metis does not access your contacts, email, files or any other part of your Google account through this connection.

How the data is used

  • Displaying your calendar. Events are shown on the Metis calendar page next to your reminders.
  • Keeping reminders in sync. Metis copies your reminders into Google Calendar. When one of those events is renamed, moved or deleted in Google, Metis makes the same change to the reminder. Metis does not change or delete any other events.
  • Spoken briefings. If you turn on the greeter, Metis can read upcoming event titles and times aloud on your own speakers.

Google data is used only for these features. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

  • Calendar events are fetched from Google when needed and are not kept in a separate copy. Synced reminders, including their titles and times, are stored in Metis's own data files on your computer.
  • The sign-in token that lets Metis reach your calendar is stored on your computer in a file only your user account can read.
  • Nothing is stored on any server run by the developer. There is no central Metis server.

Sharing

Metis does not share Google user data with anyone. The one exception is a choice you make yourself: if you set Metis to use an online text-to-speech voice (such as Microsoft Edge's voices) instead of a voice on your own computer, the text being read aloud, which can include event titles, is sent to that speech service to be turned into audio. With an offline voice, or with the greeter off, nothing leaves your computer.

Limited Use

Metis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Click Disconnect on the Metis calendar page. This revokes Metis's access with Google and deletes the stored sign-in token.
  • You can also remove Metis at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Events Metis created stay in your Google Calendar until you delete them there. Reminders stay in Metis until you delete them in Metis.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
